# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clinical staff reviewing patient narratives, AI-generated summaries, OCR documents, and followup proposals in a focused operational workspace. They need to scan risk signals, inspect full records, and record human decisions quickly and accurately.

## Product Purpose

Sayhut supports healthcare intake from patient narrative and documents through human review. AI may extract facts, interpret content, and flag risk, but no AI output is finalized automatically. Success means staff can see what needs attention, understand the source material, and approve, reject, or action work with clear status feedback.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, direct. The interface should reduce cognitive load, make urgency visible without alarm fatigue, and state development-only limitations honestly.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing landing-page patterns, decorative dashboards, ambiguous status colors, fake staff identity, and interfaces that imply AI output is authoritative or automatically finalized.

## Design Principles

- AI proposes, human disposes.
- Risk signals must be visible at the point of triage.
- Source detail should be one deliberate action away.
- Every mutation needs clear feedback and a recoverable error state.
- Development shortcuts must be labeled as shortcuts, never presented as real identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, clear labels, text alternatives for icons, reduced motion, and status messages that do not rely on color alone.
